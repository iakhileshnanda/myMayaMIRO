News Article: "Indian Market Volatility Continues Amidst Global Uncertainty"

Article Date: April 6, 2026

The Indian stock market is navigating a period of high volatility as it begins the new fiscal year (FY27), with benchmark indices Sensex and Nifty having declined over 14% each so far in 2026. This sharp correction follows a challenging FY26, which saw the market underperform global peers in what has been described as a near "black swan" event. The market's volatility index, India VIX, more than doubled to 27.89 in March, marking its biggest monthly jump in six years. This volatility is driven by a confluence of domestic and global factors:

    Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI) Outflows: Foreign investors have pulled out approximately ₹1.5 lakh crore from Indian equities in 2026 so far, including ₹19,837 crore ($2.1 billion) in the first two trading sessions of April alone. This selling pressure is attributed to rising crude oil prices, rupee weakness, and global uncertainties linked to the West Asia conflict.

    Rising Crude Oil Prices: The intensifying conflict in West Asia, particularly tensions involving the US, Iran, and Israel, has driven Brent crude prices up about 48%, raising concerns over inflation and India's current account deficit as a major oil importer.

    Geopolitical Tensions: The ongoing West Asia conflict remains an overarching factor, with reports of fresh escalations and warnings from US President Donald Trump adding to market uncertainty. Analysts suggest that any signs of de-escalation could trigger a sharp relief rally.

    Domestic Concerns: Investor focus is also on the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) bi-monthly policy scheduled for April 8. While the central bank is widely expected to keep rates unchanged, its commentary on inflation and growth will be closely watched.

    Selective Buying and Recovery Hopes: Despite the negative sentiment, there are pockets of optimism. Banking stocks saw buying interest on April 6, partly due to attractive valuations, with the Nifty Bank Index trading at its cheapest level since 2020.
