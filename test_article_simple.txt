News Article: "Reliance Industries Q4 Results Beat Expectations"

Article Date: April 5, 2026

Reliance Industries Ltd (RIL), India's largest company by market capitalization, reported strong Q4 FY26 results that beat analyst expectations. Revenue rose 12% year-on-year to Rs 2.4 lakh crore, while net profit increased 18% to Rs 19,800 crore.

Key highlights from the earnings:

Jio Platforms: The telecom arm added 15 million subscribers in Q4, bringing total users to 490 million. Average revenue per user (ARPU) rose to Rs 205 from Rs 195 in Q3, driven by tariff hikes implemented in January 2026. Jio's 5G network now covers 95% of urban India.

Retail Business: Reliance Retail reported revenue of Rs 78,000 crore, up 22% YoY. The company opened 1,200 new stores during the quarter, taking total store count to 19,500 across India. Online-to-offline integration through JioMart contributed 30% of retail revenue.

Oil-to-Chemical (O2C): The refining segment benefited from higher gross refining margins of $11.2 per barrel compared to $8.5 in the previous quarter. Rising crude oil prices due to West Asia tensions supported refining margins but raised input costs for the petrochemical division.

New Energy: RIL invested Rs 15,000 crore in its new energy business during FY26, including solar panel manufacturing at Jamnagar and battery storage partnerships. Chairman Mukesh Ambani reiterated the target of 100 GW renewable energy capacity by 2030.

Market Reaction: RIL shares rose 3.5% to Rs 2,850 in early trading on April 5, making it the top gainer on the Nifty 50. Brokerages including Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, and CLSA maintained "Buy" ratings with target prices ranging from Rs 3,100 to Rs 3,400. However, some domestic brokerages like ICICI Securities flagged concerns about rising capex in new energy potentially pressuring free cash flow in the near term.

The broader Sensex rose 1.2% on the day, partly supported by RIL's strong results. Foreign institutional investors turned net buyers for the first time in a week, purchasing Rs 2,100 crore worth of Indian equities.
